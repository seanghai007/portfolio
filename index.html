About Me

sxanghai
Phnom Penh City, Cambodia
Open for Inquiries

Visual craft grounded in intention, composition, and storytelling.

I am a multidisciplinary visual creator based in Phnom Penh City, specializing in Graphic Design as my primary focus, with dedicated practice in cinematic Video Editing and Photography.

My creative philosophy centers on restraint and clarity. Whether shaping a brand’s visual identity, pacing a narrative cut for a video, or framing an architectural space through the lens, I look for the core idea and strip away the superfluous. Every layout, color choice, and cut serves to strengthen the connection between the story and the audience.

PRINCIPLE 01

Visual Communication

Designing with hierarchy, purpose, and deliberate typographic rhythm so every message is understood instantly.

PRINCIPLE 02

Storytelling & Pacing

Structuring video sequences and brand narratives with emotional resonance, whether in 15 seconds or a long-form cut.

PRINCIPLE 03

Composition & Form

Employing balanced proportions, intentional negative space, and architectural balance across digital and physical mediums.

PRINCIPLE 04

Attention to Detail

Meticulous focus on color grading fidelity, print production tolerances, typography kerning, and pixel-level precision.
